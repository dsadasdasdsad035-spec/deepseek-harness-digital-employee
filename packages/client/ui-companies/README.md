# @deepseek-ai/dsh-client-ui-companies

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

Browser-only 3D company campus console. The sidebar footer 「组织」 dropdown (owned here, with the 数字员工 item delegating to the digital employee workspace) opens a full-screen `shell.overlay` surface with two Three.js scenes: a campus of zoned districts — four planned communities (一区..四区) inside the ring, each with a hedge boundary, a gated 小区门口 whose stub driveway plugs into the main-road network (ring + cross roads + connectors + outer grid), an internal driveway spine, and a stub path to every house's front door, houses facing their driveway and capped to the plot (roof color encodes the category, size scales with headcount, promotional image on a billboard, address on a sign) — inside a living city: dashed-lane roads, cars that wander the road graph with a random next edge at every intersection, opposite-phase green→yellow→red traffic lights, a tram, a freight train, and randomized high-speed-rail and metro runs on elevated viaducts (random direction, speed, and departure dwell each pass) with 地铁 entrance kiosks, a river with road bridges, and seeded forest clusters (all dressing, never interactive); and a roomed office interior — waist-height perimeter walls with a south double-glass entrance (大门), one partitioned office room per department along a corridor, an enclosed glass 总裁办公室 for the 总裁 department at the front, and fixed 茶水区 (counter, coffee machine, water dispenser), 休息区 (rug, facing sofas, coffee table, plants), 厕所 (stalls, sinks), and 吸烟区 (bench, ashtray) amenity areas. Employees live as NPCs inside a collision world: every fixture (desks, PCs, counters, benches, sinks) and walker registers located AABBs; idle employees wander corridor-routed paths between the amenities, detouring around furniture and yielding to colleagues; busy toggles only the head-top 在忙 label and never drives movement, so a working employee wanders on the same cadence as an idle one and lights its desk screen only while seated. A walker's detour is viable only when the whole leg from its current position is clear (endpoint-only checks let a walker adopt a detour whose entry is blocked and stand still forever), and each untraversable frame counts toward a give-up limit so a blocked walker retreats or re-plans instead of spinning in place while its path array grows. Crowding and blockages feed a runtime mood (😡 chip) and furious passers-by vandalize destructible fixtures (purely visual tilt/topple, resets on refresh); every amenity arrival reports into the employee's durable visit history (`whereabouts.json` via the group gateway — proof each instance persists across refreshes), and each desk computer is bound to its employee, painting their latest real session messages beside the busy screen. Walls are solid: every room partition, the CEO glass, the perimeter, and the entrance door panels register as blocking segments with passable door gaps — NPCs walk through doors only, slides and retreats stay inside rooms, and their own desk/PC never blocks them. A walk home is routed door-aware on both sides (inside and outside the room each advance monotonically toward the target), so a walker outside the CEO office crosses at its door gap instead of cutting through the glass. Positions persist across visits: car fleet states and employee floor coordinates snapshot durably (`companies/world-state.json` through the management remotes) — existing cars and employees resume from their last coordinates, and only first appearances spawn (cars random, employees at their desk). Cars follow a same-edge car-following gap plus an oncoming hold so traffic queues without pass-through in either direction; deep department rows open sideways into a widened inter-row corridor. Each company carries an isolated `skinId` selecting one of five procedural skins (现代简约 default, 中式庭院, 科技霓虹, 田园木屋, 玻璃幕墙); the skin drives both layers — house form, palette, and decorations on the campus; interior slab, walls, furniture, desks, and lighting inside — and switching it in the 公司皮肤 picker hot-swaps the active scene without a page reload. Employees show 在忙 or 空闲 on their head-top label regardless of position (chat-sourced state rides the live WebSocket session status bits; task-sourced verdicts refresh through a 5-second floor poll); the label is presentation only and does not affect movement. `three` is inlined into the client bundle (no GLTF assets — all geometry is procedural). Clicking a seated employee opens a detail panel with a jump to the digital employee workspace; clicking an empty desk opens the bind picker for that department.

This package also owns the company group conversation surface and the group composer's `@` source: in a group session the picker lists that company's bound members (read through the group gateway's `listCompanyGroupMembers`, the same roster the Lead's mention router matches) and inserts a plain `@displayName` mention — never a routing reference — so the Lead's deterministic router delivers it unchanged.

The console rides `shell.overlay` (additive) rather than `shell.application` (single seat owned by the digital employee workspace, which a second registrant could only shadow).

## Model Experience

None, as this package renders the console; it contributes no prompt content, tools, or session events.

#### KV Cache effect

None; console state never enters a model request.

## Known Limitations and Deferred Work

- **No per-seat camera framing** — clicking an employee opens the detail panel without animating the camera to their desk.
- **Billboard textures load lazily** — promo images pop in after the first campus render.
